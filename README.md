# Featured projects

## [EntryDesk](https://github.com/pungyman/EntryDesk)

**ACM ICAIF 2026 · Trading Agent Competition entry**

Can an AI choose a portfolio—and show its work before buying it? Four specialist agents turn market data, daily ensemble ranks, volatility forecasts, and news into an initial allocation. Code quantifies its risks, the portfolio manager gets one chance to revise, and a validated decision becomes the starting book.

[![Graphical evidence streams feed four analyst agents, whose independent reports inform the portfolio manager and its stock weights. Code provides bounded risk review and validation.](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pungyman/EntryDesk/main/assets/entry-desk-flow.svg)](https://github.com/pungyman/EntryDesk/blob/main/docs/entry-mechanism.md)

## [BeyondAccuracy](https://github.com/pungyman/BeyondAccuracy)

**Accepted at Multi-LLL 2026 · AACL-IJCNLP workshop**

Can an LLM become more accurate while getting worse at the task? We study language subspaces, activation interventions, and multilingual reasoning across four open-weight models. Evaluating correctness together with response language reveals trade-offs that accuracy alone hides.

## [DeepWheat](https://github.com/pungyman/DeepWheat)

**Published in [Scientific Reports](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-56576-5) · 2026**

When does last year’s harvest stop being a good forecast? DeepWheat combines satellite observations, weather, and soil profiles to predict wheat yields across 275 Indian districts. Its clearest advantage over simply repeating last year’s yield emerges in a dry season, when changing conditions make history less reliable.

[![Observed wheat yields, DeepWheat predictions, and prediction errors across India](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pungyman/DeepWheat/HEAD/assets/wheat-yield-maps.png)](https://github.com/pungyman/DeepWheat)
